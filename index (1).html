How the course is built

The course (first assessed in 2025) is organised as a grid. Four themes run across four levels of biological organisation, so every topic has a code such as C1.1: theme C, level 1 (molecules), first topic. Read across a row to see one level through every theme, or down a column to follow one big idea from molecules to ecosystems.

Standard level and higher level

Standard level (SL)

150 teaching hours. You study the SL content of every topic in all four themes.

Paper 1 lasts 1 hour 30 minutes; Paper 2 lasts 1 hour 30 minutes.

Higher level (HL)

240 teaching hours. You study everything at SL plus additional higher level (AHL) content, including six topics that are HL only.

Paper 1 lasts 2 hours; Paper 2 lasts 2 hours 30 minutes.

How your final grade is made up

Paper 1 36%
Paper 2 44%
IA 20%

The same weightings apply at SL and HL. Your teacher will confirm exact mark totals from the current IB Biology guide.

Skills that run through every topic

Experimental techniques

Measuring variables, using microscopes and data loggers, applying safe and ethical practice.

Applying mathematics

Means, standard deviation, percentage change, rates, scale and magnification, and statistical tests.

Inquiry process

Exploring and designing, collecting and processing data, concluding and evaluating.

Nature of science

How evidence, models, falsification and peer review shape biological knowledge.

Collaborative sciences project

An interdisciplinary project with students from other sciences. It is required but not graded.

Linking questions

Each topic ends with questions that connect it to other themes. Use them in Paper 2 extended responses.

A typical biological pattern you will meet

External assessment

Two written papers make up 80% of your grade. Learn what each section looks like, read worked examples, then practise each question style.

Internal assessment

The scientific investigation is your own experiment, written up as a report and marked by your teacher against four criteria. It is worth 20% of your grade at both SL and HL.

About 10 hours

of class time for planning, collecting data and writing.

3,000 words maximum

Tables, graphs, equations and references do not count towards this, but extended prose inside them does.

24 marks

Four criteria, each marked out of 6 using best-fit bands.

The four criteria

Select a criterion to see what examiners look for, compare weak and strong writing, and check your own draft.
